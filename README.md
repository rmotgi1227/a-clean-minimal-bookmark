# a-clean-minimal-bookmark

A clean, minimal bookmark manager: save links with tags, search instantly, all in your browser.

Wished by TyrantRC and asked for by people who wanted tags instead of folders, one place for their collection, and search that actually works.

## How to use

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There is nothing to install, no server, and no accounts — your bookmarks stay in your browser's local storage. Paste a link, add tags, press Save, then search by title, URL, or tag.
